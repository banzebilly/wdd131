Our story

A studio built on the belief that beauty is a quiet practice.

My journey in beauty started with a passion for helping people feel confident through professional nail and beauty treatments. After gaining experience in a busy salon, I decided to start my own business, Billy Salon, where I could offer personalised services and build lasting relationships with my clients.

Starting my own salon allowed me to grow not only as a beauty therapist but also as a business owner. I focused on providing high-quality treatments, maintaining excellent hygiene standards, and creating a warm, welcoming environment where every client felt comfortable and valued.

Over the years, I have earned the trust of more than 200 loyal clients through quality work, attention to detail, and genuine customer care. Every appointment has helped me grow both professionally and personally.
